ABOUT THE PROJECT

Subscription clarity.

RenewTrack helps people stay aware of recurring payments and upcoming renewals.

Project overview

People often manage multiple digital subscriptions across streaming, cloud storage, education and productivity platforms. RenewTrack provides one place to record subscription details, monitor renewal dates, store invoice files and understand recurring costs.

Technology stack

PythonFlaskSQLiteHTML5CSS3Jinja2

Core modules

  • Subscription CRUD (create, read, update and delete)
  • Renewal date tracking and upcoming-renewal dashboard
  • Invoice upload and retrieval
  • Monthly and annual spending analytics

This is a local academic demo. It uses sample records on first launch; you can edit or delete them and add your own data.
